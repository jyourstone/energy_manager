**Algorithm steps (port from AppDaemon `home_battery_manager.py`):**

1. **Input validation:** If price_slots empty, return idle result. Convert price slot dicts to internal representation.

2. **Classify slots by price:** Compare each slot's price against charge_threshold and discharge_threshold to identify candidate charge and discharge slots.

3. **Peak grouping (`_group_into_peaks`):** Group consecutive discharge-candidate slots into "peaks." If two groups are separated by more than `peak_gap_hours`, they are separate peaks. Each peak = a list of discharge slots with a total potential discharge energy.

4. **Virtual energy tracking:** Starting from current_soc_pct, simulate the battery through time:
   - For each peak, calculate how much energy the battery can discharge (limited by current virtual SOC and min_soc_pct).
   - Between peaks, identify the cheapest available charge slots to refill the battery (limited by max_soc_pct and max_charge_power_w).
   - Track virtual SOC through the entire schedule.

5. **Solar adjustment:** If solar_forecast_wh is provided, distribute estimated production across daylight hours (simplified: 6:00-18:00 UTC+1, even distribution). During hours with expected solar production, reduce the number of grid charge slots needed. Mark slots where solar covers charging as "solar_charge" instead of "charge".

6. **Build final schedule:** Assign each slot an action (charge/discharge/idle/solar_charge) based on the optimization.

7. **Derive current state:** Find the slot containing `now` and set current_action. Map to target_ems_mode:
   - "charge" or "solar_charge" -> "command_charging"
   - "discharge" -> "max_self_consumption"
   - "idle" -> "standby"

8. **Derive next slots:** Scan forward from `now` to find next_charging_slot and next_discharging_slot.

**Helper functions to implement:**

- `_group_into_peaks(discharge_candidates: list, gap_hours: float) -> list[list]` -- Groups discharge slots separated by gaps.
- `_find_cheapest_charge_slots(candidates: list, energy_needed_kwh: float, max_power_w: float) -> list` -- Selects cheapest available charge slots to meet energy need.
- `_calculate_slot_energy_kwh(max_power_w: float, duration_hours: float) -> float` -- Energy in one slot at given power.
- `_find_current_slot(schedule: list[ScheduleSlot], now: datetime) -> ScheduleSlot | None` -- Binary search for slot containing `now`.
- `_action_to_ems_mode(action: str) -> str` -- Maps scheduler action to EMS mode string.

**Important constraints:**
- All datetimes must be UTC-aware. Use `datetime.now(timezone.utc)` as default for `now` parameter.
- No HA imports. Only stdlib (datetime, dataclasses) and typing.
- Price slots are dicts with "start" (datetime), "end" (datetime), "price" (float) keys -- matching PriceSlot format from coordinator.py.
- Slot duration is derived from end-start (typically 1 hour but don't hardcode).
